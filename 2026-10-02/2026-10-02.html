PAMI: Part Anchored Motion for Text to Human-Object Interaction Generation

Chuqiao Li, Xianghui Xie, Yong Cao, Andreas Geiger, Gerard Pons-Moll

motion

10/01 12:00

Text-conditioned full-body human-object interaction (HOI) generation requires synthesizing human motion and object trajectories that match the input text while remaining precisely coordinated over time. Most methods represent the human and object as separate trajectories and predict the global human-object couplings.
